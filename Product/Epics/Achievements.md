# Epic - Achievements

Deliver privacy-conscious, first-party, read-only achievement progress with
trustworthy identity, offline caching, and explicit provider attribution.

## Workstreams

- Achievement domain model and stable identity
- First-party provider contract
- Steam achievement retrieval and mapping
- Atomic bounded cache and stale/offline policy
- Game-details presentation and accessibility
- Privacy, cancellation, malformed-response, and rate-limit testing
