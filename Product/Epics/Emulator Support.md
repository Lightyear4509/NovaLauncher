# Epic - Emulator Support

## Alpha MVP

- Emulator profile contract
- RetroArch
- Dolphin
- PCSX2
- RPCS3
- Automatic ROM scanner with dry-run
- Stable hashes and duplicate detection
- Launch actions and per-game overrides
- Metadata and artwork pipeline reuse
- RetroAchievements mapping
- Save discovery hook
- BIOS/firmware status without distribution

## Later profiles

- Ryujinx-compatible forks
- Citra-compatible forks
- Xenia
- Additional community profiles

NovaLauncher will not download ROMs, BIOS files, keys, or copyrighted firmware.
