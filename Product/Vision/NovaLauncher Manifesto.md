# NovaLauncher Manifesto

> "One launcher. Every game. Your way."

---

# Why NovaLauncher Exists

Gaming today is fragmented.

One game is on Steam.

Another is on Epic.

Another is on GOG.

Another needs an emulator.

Another needs a mod manager.

Players shouldn't have to think about where a game lives before they can play it.

NovaLauncher exists to remove those barriers.

The launcher should disappear into the background so players can focus on gaming.

---

# Our Mission

Build the most beautiful, fastest, and most customizable game launcher ever created.

Not because it has the most features.

Because every feature feels intentional.

---

# Our Vision

NovaLauncher should become the launcher people recommend first—not because they have to, but because they genuinely enjoy using it.

It should feel modern.

Fast.

Reliable.

Open.

Fun.

---

# Core Values

## Speed

Everything should feel instant.

Animations should be smooth.

Loading should be invisible.

Searching should be immediate.

---

## Simplicity

The UI should never overwhelm the user.

Powerful doesn't mean complicated.

Advanced features should remain accessible without cluttering the interface.

---

## Customization

Every player uses their PC differently.

NovaLauncher should adapt to the user—not force the user to adapt to the launcher.

Themes.

Layouts.

Plugins.

Everything should be customizable.

---

## Community

NovaLauncher belongs to everyone.

Plugins should be easy to create.

Themes should be easy to share.

The project should welcome contributions.

---

## Quality

Every release should improve the experience.

Never ship something because "it's good enough."

Polish matters.

Consistency matters.

Details matter.

---

# Design Philosophy

When building a feature ask:

Does this make gaming easier?

Does this remove friction?

Does it make the launcher feel faster?

Can it be customized?

Can someone understand it immediately?

If not...

Redesign it.

---

# Architecture Philosophy

Architecture exists to make future development easier.

Every feature should fit naturally into the project.

Avoid shortcuts that create technical debt.

Build systems that can grow.

---

# User Experience

Opening NovaLauncher should feel exciting.

It should immediately show your library.

Your artwork should look amazing.

Your games should launch instantly.

Nothing should distract from playing.

---

# Open Source Philosophy

NovaLauncher should always be:

Transparent

Well documented

Easy to contribute to

Friendly to new developers

Built with maintainability in mind

---

# Success

NovaLauncher succeeds when:

Players stop thinking about launchers.

Developers enjoy contributing.

Themes flourish.

Plugins flourish.

Communities form around customization.

The launcher becomes something people love using every day.

---

# Final Statement

NovaLauncher is more than software.

It's a platform for bringing every game together.

Every decision should move us closer to that vision.
